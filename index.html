✨ Features

Capture Your Thoughts

Express your thoughts and feelings in a safe, private space. Writing helps you process emotions and gain clarity on what's on your mind. Your journal entries are protected with end-to-end encryption.

Self-Care Balance (Semaphore System)

Check in with your energy levels using our semaphore system. Learn to recognize when you're giving too much and need to prioritize self-care. Track your energy patterns over time to maintain healthy balance.

CBT Exercises

Use Cognitive Behavioral Therapy techniques to explore your emotions, identify triggers, and understand the connections between your thoughts and feelings. Build healthier mental habits with guided exercises.

Emotion Tracking & Insights

Monitor your emotional intensity with intuitive tracking tools. Visualize mood patterns over time, identify triggers, and gain deep insights into your emotional landscape through our analytics dashboard.

Connect with Mental Health Specialists

Find qualified therapists, psychologists, and counselors who understand your needs. Message specialists securely within the app for professional support when you need it most.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start using the app?

After downloading the app, create an account and you'll be guided through the welcome tutorial. Start with "Capture Your Thought" to begin your healing journey.

Is my data secure?

Yes! Your thoughts and reflections are protected with end-to-end encryption. Only you have access to your encrypted content.

Can I recover my data if I forget my password?

Due to our zero-knowledge encryption design, we cannot recover your encrypted data if you lose your password. Please keep your password safe and consider using a password manager.

How do I connect with a specialist?

Navigate to the Specialist Support section from your home screen to browse available mental health professionals and send secure messages.

How often should I use the app?

We recommend daily check-ins to build a consistent wellness practice, but use the app at whatever frequency works best for you. The app tracks your progress and streaks to help maintain consistency.

🔐 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

⚠️
Your Privacy Matters
Healing Journal is built on a foundation of privacy and security. We use end-to-end encryption to protect your most personal thoughts and reflections.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and password (encrypted)
  • Journal Content: Your thoughts, reflections, and notes (end-to-end encrypted)
  • Progress Data: Exercise completion, emotion tracking data, and self-care check-ins
  • Usage Analytics: App usage patterns to improve features (anonymized)

2. End-to-End Encryption

Your personal thoughts and journal reflections are protected with end-to-end encryption:

  • Content is encrypted on your device before being sent to our servers
  • Only you have the encryption keys to access your content
  • We cannot read, access, or recover your encrypted journal entries
  • Your encryption keys are derived from your password and never stored on our servers
🔑
Important: Keep your password safe. If you lose it, we cannot recover your encrypted data due to our zero-knowledge security design.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Healing Journal service
  • To enable features like emotion tracking, progress insights, and calendar views
  • To facilitate secure communication with mental health specialists
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To send important service updates and notifications (with your permission)

4. What We DON'T Do

  • We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do NOT use your journal content for advertising
  • We do NOT share your encrypted content with anyone, including mental health specialists, without your explicit consent
  • We do NOT track your activity across other websites or apps

5. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted over secure HTTPS connections
  • Data stored on secure, encrypted servers
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and monitoring to prevent unauthorized access

6. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • You can delete individual journal entries, exercises, or your entire account at any time
  • Deleted data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days
  • Encrypted content cannot be recovered after deletion

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our app:

  • Supabase: Backend database and authentication services
  • Analytics: Anonymized usage analytics to improve the app

These services are bound by strict data protection agreements and do not have access to your encrypted journal content.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Withdraw consent at any time

9. Children's Privacy

Healing Journal is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email. Your continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
