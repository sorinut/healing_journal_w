Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use end-to-end encryption (E2EE) for all your sensitive data. Your thoughts are encrypted on your device before reaching our servers. Only you can decrypt and read this content.

What happens if I forget my password?

Due to our end-to-end encryption, we cannot recover your encrypted data if you forget your password. Please store your password securely. You can create a new account, but previous encrypted data will be inaccessible.

Can I access my data on multiple devices?

Yes! Log in with the same account on any device, and your encrypted data will sync automatically. You'll need to enter your password on each device to decrypt your data.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your profile menu → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account. Your deletion request will be processed within 72 hours, and all your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

Is this app a replacement for therapy?

No. This app is a supportive tool for mental wellness and self-reflection. It is not a substitute for professional mental health treatment. If you're experiencing severe symptoms, please consult a licensed mental health professional.

How much does the app cost?

The app is currently free during our initial launch period. We may introduce premium features in the future, but core functionality will remain accessible.
