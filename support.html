Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use end-to-end encryption (E2EE) for all your sensitive data. Your thoughts are encrypted on your device before reaching our servers. Only you can decrypt and read this content. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.

What happens if I forget my password?

Due to our end-to-end encryption, we cannot recover your encrypted data if you forget your password. Please store your password securely. You can create a new account, but previous encrypted data will be inaccessible.

Can I access my data on multiple devices?

Yes! Log in with the same account on any device, and your encrypted data will sync automatically. You'll need to enter your password on each device to decrypt your data.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your profile menu → Privacy & Data → Delete My Account. Your deletion request will be processed within 72 hours, and all your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

Is this app a replacement for therapy?

No. Healing Journal is NOT a substitute for professional therapy or mental health treatment.

This app is designed as a supportive tool for:

  • Self-reflection and journaling
  • Emotion tracking and awareness
  • Evidence-based wellness activities (breathing exercises, grounding techniques)
  • Self-care routines

What this app does NOT do:

  • Provide medical or clinical advice
  • Diagnose mental health conditions
  • Treat or cure mental health disorders
  • Replace professional therapy or counseling

⚠️ If you're experiencing severe symptoms, please consult a licensed mental health professional.

What should I do if I'm in crisis or having thoughts of self-harm?

🚨 If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, suicidal thoughts, or thoughts of self-harm, please seek immediate help. This app is not designed for crisis intervention.

Please contact one of these resources immediately:

🌍 International Crisis Hotlines:

  • USA: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) or call 1-800-273-8255
  • UK: Samaritans - 116 123 (free 24/7)
  • Canada: Talk Suicide Canada - 1-833-456-4566 (24/7)
  • Australia: Lifeline - 13 11 14 (24/7)
  • EU: 112 (emergency services)
  • International: findahelpline.com

📞 Emergency Services: Call your local emergency number (911, 112, 999, etc.) or go to your nearest emergency room if you are in immediate danger.

What about the mental health specialists in the app?

Our app includes a directory of verified mental health specialists (psychologists, therapists, counselors) to help you find professional support. However, any therapy or treatment you receive through connecting with these specialists occurs outside of the app. The app facilitates the connection but does not provide or replace professional clinical services.

How much does the app cost?

The app is currently free during our initial launch period. We may introduce premium features in the future, but core functionality will remain accessible.
